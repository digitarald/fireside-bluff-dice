How to bluff

  1. Roll, then Reveal to peek — keep the screen to yourself.
  2. Announce a score. True or a lie, it must beat the last call.
  3. Pass the cup hidden. The next player either believes you and rolls, or calls the bluff with Reveal.
  4. Caught lying, or wrongly calling a bluff? You lose the round.
